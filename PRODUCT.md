# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Freight decision-makers** (supply-chain managers, procurement leads at manufacturers/retailers/F&B) evaluating a 3PL partner for UAE/GCC lanes and cold chain storage. They arrive skeptical — logistics sites are usually cluttered and interchangeable — and decide in seconds whether GSL feels like a serious, modern operator.
- **Shippers with recurring volume** comparing GSL against legacy forwarders; they care about reliability, tracking/visibility technology, temperature integrity and certified quality.
- **Talent** (drivers, warehouse ops, engineers) landing on Career.

Job to be done: understand what GSL moves and stores, where, and why it's more reliable than the incumbent — then get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for **Global Shipping & Logistics (L.L.C)** — a Dubai **3PL, part of Al Shirawi Group**, with freight-forwarding roots since 1975. Road transport is the visual hero story; the offering spans international freight, customs clearance, port haulage, **cold chain warehousing (ambient to −25°C)**, distribution, value-added services and stocktaking/merchandising. Trust anchors: LRQA-audited, UKAS-accredited ISO 9001/14001/22000/45001 + BRCGS ("the region's only 3PL certified across all five"). Tagline: **"Driven by You."** Success = qualified contact-form leads and the immediate impression of a premium, technology-driven operator.

## Brand Personality

**Cinematic. Engineered. Assured.**
The voice of a company that moves physical mass across continents at night and makes it look effortless. Confident, low word-count, no exclamation marks. Luxury expressed through precision and restraint — the feel of a night highway: dark, calm, purposeful motion, headlights cutting through.

## Anti-references

- **Old-school freight sites**: cluttered corporate logistics pages, stock handshake photos, blue headers, dense link farms, "ISO-certified since 1987" energy.
- **Generic SaaS landing**: cream backgrounds, gradient text, purple gradients, identical icon-card grids, hero-metric templates — the saturated AI-template look.
- Maritime-first framing: ships may appear as a supporting mode, never as the identity.

## Design Principles

1. **Night-highway cinema** — the dark canvas is the brand. Motion, light, and depth do the storytelling; copy stays short.
2. **Show the journey, don't list features** — scroll is a trip along a freight corridor; sections are waypoints, transitions are travel.
3. **Precision is the luxury** — data details (route codes, coordinates, timestamps in mono) signal engineering competence; nothing decorative that couldn't appear on a real telematics screen.
4. **Motion with mass** — animations carry the weight of freight: exponential ease-outs, no bounce, nothing weightless or gimmicky. Every effect has a reduced-motion fallback.
5. **One accent, used like headlights** — amber cuts the dark sparingly: CTAs, route arcs, live indicators. If everything glows, nothing does.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 on dark surfaces, large display ≥3:1.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` gets a complete experience (crossfades, static globe frame, no scroll-jacking) — not a broken one.
- All scroll-driven content must be present and readable without JS/animation; reveals enhance visible defaults.
- Keyboard: focus trap in modal and mobile nav, visible focus states on dark.
